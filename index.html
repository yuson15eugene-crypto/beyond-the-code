About the Project

A Human Side to Computer Science.

Beyond the Code is a student interview project focused on the real experiences of second-year, third-year, and fourth-year Computer Science students at Lipa City Colleges. It aims to share their perspectives, challenges, personal growth, and advice with the wider student community.

Stories and quotations should be based on actual interviews and published with the interviewees' permission.
